RESEARCH

Decoding Drug Off-Targets

Drugs can interact with proteins beyond their intended therapeutic targets. At iMBL, we aim to understand the molecular basis of these interactions, predict potential off-target liabilities, quantify uncertainty in those predictions, and explore how this knowledge can support improved molecular selectivity and safer therapeutics.

OUR LONG-TERM QUESTIONS

Questions that guide iMBL

01

Where do drugs go beyond their intended target?

We aim to identify and prioritize plausible unintended drug–protein interactions across proteins and protein families, particularly those that may influence therapeutic safety, efficacy, or variability in response.

02

Why do off-target interactions occur?

We investigate the structural, chemical, and dynamic determinants that allow a molecule optimized for one target to recognize another, including binding-site similarity, conformational plasticity, molecular recognition, and the energetic basis of selectivity and promiscuity.

03

Which predictions can we trust?

We develop and evaluate predictive models that explicitly consider uncertainty, probability calibration, and applicability domain, with the goal of separating confident predictions from cases where additional evidence is required.

04

Can we improve molecular selectivity?

By identifying the molecular features associated with selectivity and promiscuity, we aim to support the prioritization and design of compounds with reduced off-target liability and improved therapeutic safety.

HOW WE APPROACH THESE QUESTIONS

Methods that connect prediction with molecular mechanism

01

Molecular modeling & structural bioinformatics

Protein structures, binding-site comparison, molecular recognition, ligand–protein interactions, and structure-based hypotheses of selectivity.

02

Molecular dynamics & simulation

Conformational dynamics, interaction stability, mechanistic interpretation, membrane systems, and energetic characterization of molecular interactions.

03

AI/ML & cheminformatics

Molecular representations, chemical similarity, drug–target prediction, off-target prioritization, and data-driven modeling of molecular interactions.

04

Bayesian & uncertainty-aware modeling

Probabilistic prediction, uncertainty quantification, calibration, applicability-domain analysis, and decision-making under uncertainty.
